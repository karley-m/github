untill next time!

picnic time!

shortly after this photo was taken, a giant rhino beetle landed on my brother's shirt. we think it was because of how brightly colored he was. he was technically in his bathing suit because we were getting ready to go to the lake. beside him is my little cousin and my aunt. when the beetle landed on him, no one dared to move. only when it started crawling up his shoulder did someone gently poke it and we watched it fly away.

out on the lake

our go-to spot to camp is sugar pine point in lake tahoe. it's about a 5 minute drive from the lake itself. the bottom picture is my brother and i hanging out in our trusty yellow tube. the water is always so cold. in the top photo, i went far out by the buoy. i had to go back because i get freaked out being in deep water. when you look down, the water is so clear you can see all the way to the bottom.

first bear encounter!

this is a different bear from the photo because we saw two bears that day, a little black one and a big brown one. this guy walked right behind our tents as we were packing up to leave that morning. i love bears, but i got a little nervous. we were worried about him being so close to the road. thankfully, a park ranger showed up shortly after and the bear got to a nice, safe spot.

i wanted to be a park ranger at one point

i don't even remember taking this photo. isn't it kind of odd that smokey bear doesn't wear a shirt but commits to pants, a belt, and a hat?

climbing this mountain

this was a fire lookout called buck rock and it's 172 steps to get up to it. i bought a shirt to commemorate this feat. i will likely never do it again.
