War!

Arrrr Matey, ready for a game of war?

Instructions:

  • Grab two players and choose avatars.
  • Shuffle the deck and split it in half.
  • For every round, click ‘Flip Card’ to flip the top cards for each player.
  • The person with the highest card each round collects loot.
  • If both players flip the same card, shout ‘war!’ and place another card down, and one face up. Whoever has the highest card collects double.
  • Play until one player has collected all of the cards. They are the winner!
