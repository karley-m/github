War!

Arrrr Matey, ready for a game of war?

Instructions:

  • Grab two players and choose avatars.
  • Shuffle the deck and split it in half (13 cards each).
  • For every round, click ‘Flip Card’ to flip the top cards for each player.
  • The person with the highest card each round collects the sum of the cards flipped.
  • If both players flip the same card, war happens! Click flip cards to see who wins.
  • Play until all cards are used up in the 13 rounds. Player with the most points win!
